# Product

## Product Summary

Lyfe app helps keep track of your life admin work, on a cadence type schedule. How often do you need new tires or tires rotated? How about oil changes? Doctor's appointments? Financial advisor meetings? Home upkeep/repairs? 

## Problem

Life is endlessly complicated and complex these days. Keeping it all in your head isn't a viable option. Rather than try to remember it, ro set reminders, Lyfe lets you enter the info you want/need and a cadence of reminders. Need new tires every 2 years? You probably don't remember the exact brand or mileage you put them on the car. Enter/track that info here, and scale the concept out to other life arenas.

## Target Users

Busy professional adults who don't have a butler to keep track of things but have numerous responsibilities. 

## Core User Outcomes

Track, maintain life, set up rhythms to keep from turning small bumps into roadblocks. Better to fix the tire now than replace them ALL later because you forgot.
